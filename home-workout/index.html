8-week progression

Prioritise good technique over heavier weights

Weeks 1–4 · Build the base

Start with a manageable load. Learn each movement and finish sets with 2–3 good reps still possible.

Weeks 5–8 · Progress gradually

When every set reaches the top of its rep range with clean form, increase dumbbell weight slightly.

Training essentials

⏱ Rest 60–90 seconds

Rest 90–120 seconds after demanding leg and row sets if needed.

🎯 Choose the right load

Use a weight you can control through the full range without swinging.

🛌 Recover well

Sleep, hydrate and eat enough protein to support muscle recovery.

Stop if you feel sharp pain, dizziness or unusual discomfort. If you are new to strength training, begin with 2 sets per exercise and progress only when recovery and technique are good.
